<strong>Current Team Members:</strong>

<strong><em>Keith Kintigh</em></strong>, Arizona State University&nbsp; &lt;[kintigh@asu.edu](mailto:kintigh@asu.edu)&gt; Kintigh is Professor Emeritus of Archaeology at Arizona State University. <em><strong>Tim Kohler</strong></em>, Washington State University &lt;[tako@wsu.edu](mailto:tako@wsu.edu)&gt; Kohler is Regents' Professor Emeritus of Anthropology at WSU, an external professor at the <a href='https://www.santafe.edu/' target="_blank" rel="noopener">Santa Fe Institute,</a> and a Research Associate at <a href='http://ccac.org' target="_blank" rel="noopener">Crow Canyon Archaeological Center</a>, Cortez.
